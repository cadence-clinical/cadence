Sidebar: collapsible application navigation, with shadcn's part names. Items follow the density, the page shown is marked by a bar and `aria-current` as well as its fill, and a long name wraps. On a phone it is a sheet built on Dialog. Collapsed off the canvas, its links are hidden from the keyboard and screen readers. Ctrl+B and Cmd+B toggle it, except while focus is in a field.
