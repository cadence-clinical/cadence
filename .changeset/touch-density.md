A touch device that has not set `data-density` gets a touch default between compact and comfortable: 40px controls, 14px control and body text, 16px titles and 12px padding. It had been comfortable, with 44px controls and 15px text. Set `data-density="comfortable"` for 44px targets. A large control is 44px at the touch default.
