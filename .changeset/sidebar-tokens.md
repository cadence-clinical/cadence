Sidebar tokens. `sidebar` is the sidebar's surface. shadcn's seven other sidebar tokens, such as `sidebar-foreground` and `sidebar-primary`, are aliases of the Cadence tokens they correspond to, exported as `SIDEBAR_ALIASES`, so they follow every mode, contrast level, accent and brand. The registry theme carries them. New contrast pairings cover text and the focus ring on `sidebar`, a field on it, and the shown item's bar and focus ring on its fill.
