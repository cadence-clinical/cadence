Vitals chart: the pointer over a value opens its Tooltip with its details, and elsewhere draws a line for timing with the time in words beside it. The span and range choices are Tabs. A `totals` track shows each round's total in its level's colour. Track chart: marks take a `detail` for their Tooltip, the chart takes `now` for the time in words, and a band open at an end runs to the track's edge. Adds `friendlyTime`.
