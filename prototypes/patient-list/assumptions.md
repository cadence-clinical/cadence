# Patient list: assumptions

Each assumption is confirmed, rejected or open. An open assumption names who can confirm it.

| #   | Assumption                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Why it matters                                                                                            | Status                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Confirmed by, date          |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| A1  | Doctors (interns, residents, registrars) use and edit the list, consultants oversee it, and nurses and allied health use its outputs.                                                                             | Decides whose needs come first and who edits.                                                             | Confirmed. Nurses also complete jobs within their scope, and a clinical nurse can assign a patient to a team (round 2)                                                                                                                                          | Maintainer, 2026-10-18      |
| A2  | A list is a unit's list, a team's list, or an audit list built from criteria.                                                                                                                                     | Decides what membership means and what "live" changes.                                                    | Confirmed: unit, team, audit and private personal lists                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Maintainer, 2026-10-18      |
| A3  | It is used at the bedside on shared workstations and at desks, and phones are secondary.                                                                                                                          | Decides layout, density and touch targets.                                                                | Confirmed, with phones for fast ward rounds and reference on the go                                                                                                                                                                                             | Maintainer, 2026-10-18      |
| A4  | "Live" means everyone on the same list sees its patients, order and annotations change as others edit.                                                                                                            | Decides what the sync layer shares, and what conflicts can happen.                                        | Open                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Maintainer                  |
| A5  | The data comes from Oracle Health Millennium's FHIR R4 APIs, as the synthetic data decision says.                                                                                                                 | Decides which fields can be shown at all.                                                                 | Partly checked: Oracle's R4 docs (2026.04.23) serve Patient, Encounter, Location, CareTeam, Condition, AllergyIntolerance, Observation, DiagnosticReport, DocumentReference and MedicationRequest, but not List, Task, Flag, EpisodeOfCare or PractitionerRole. | Research report, 2026-10-18 |
| A6  | A list's membership is held by the app, not the EHR: Oracle has no List or Group resource, and an Encounter can't be searched by location, so it can't produce a ward census. Patients are added to a list by id. | Decides how a ward list is filled and kept current.                                                       | Open                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Maintainer                  |
| A7  | The jobs, comments and seen-marks a ward round produces live in the app's own store, shared by the sync layer: Oracle has no Task or Flag, and Communication can't be searched by patient.                        | Decides what is lost or kept if the app isn't the record, and whether anything is written back as a note. | Open                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Maintainer                  |
| A8  | Whether text was AI-drafted is recorded in the app layer: Oracle's documented Provenance has no device agent or activity to say so.                                                                               | Applies the AI provenance rule to this data source.                                                       | Open                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Maintainer                  |
| A9  | The head of unit's view of patient mix, acuity and workload belongs to the unit dashboard feature, built on the same unit, team and patient relationships as the list.                                            | Decides the patient list's scope.                                                                         | Open                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Maintainer                  |
| A10 | An audit list's criteria are chosen by the person who builds it and are always shown with the list, so a cohort never looks like Cadence judged the patients (guardrail 1).                                       | Keeps audit lists on the right side of the clinical boundary.                                             | Confirmed (round 3 default)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Maintainer, 2026-10-18      |
| A11 | A patient removed from an audit list by hand stays removed even if they still meet its criteria, and the removal shows who removed them and when.                                                                 | Decides how "stay up to date" and "remove" combine.                                                       | Open                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Maintainer                  |
| A12 | A job records which roles can complete it, so the list can show a nurse which jobs are within their scope.                                                                                                        | Decides how "within their scope" appears on screen.                                                       | Open                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Maintainer                  |
| A13 | Flags are labels a unit or team defines, such as "for discharge" or "isolation", applied by a clinician. Cadence ships no flag that implies a clinical judgement of its own.                                      | Keeps flags user-defined (guardrail 1), and decides who defines them.                                     | Confirmed (round 3 default)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Maintainer, 2026-10-18      |
| A14 | A patient's history in the list system records referral, acceptance, assignment, each transfer with its handover note, flags, acuity changes and jobs, each with who and when.                                    | Decides what the history view shows.                                                                      | Open                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Maintainer                  |
| A15 | Discharge is its own feature, and printing a list is out of scope for this prototype.                                                                                                                             | Bounds the prototype.                                                                                     | Open: round 3 answered only for shift handover                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Maintainer                  |
