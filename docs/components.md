# Component plan

The components Cadence intends to build, by [level](decisions/0013-component-levels.md), in build order. This is a working document: edit it. A component is built only when its status is **agreed**.

| Status   | Means                                                       |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Built    | Merged or in an open pull request                           |
| Agreed   | The maintainer has asked for it, or approved it             |
| Proposed | Suggested, with the reason. Not built until agreed          |
| Planned  | Intended, and agreed one at a time when its turn comes      |
| Deferred | Agreed in principle, and set aside until something needs it |

The maintainer approved this plan on 2026-09-20, and asked for Skeleton, Marker, Slider, Collapsible, Empty, Sheet, Drawer, Calendar, Date picker and Toast on 2026-09-22. On 2026-09-23 the maintainer agreed the Observation table, the Vitals chart, the Medication card and the Track chart they are built on. On 2026-10-18 the maintainer asked for the components five prototyped features need to be proposed: see [Features being prototyped](#features-being-prototyped). On 2026-10-19 the maintainer agreed Breadcrumb, Avatar and the Application shell's inset layout, for the prototypes' `sidebar-08` layout. Later that day, for round 3 of the patient list, the maintainer agreed Status and Comment, and rows in the Data table that open on a click. For round 4 the maintainer asked for rows of two or three lines in the Data table, a bar that marks its chosen row, and comments that can be edited briefly and then only revoked. For round 5 the maintainer asked for a Mode toggle, the first way in Cadence to choose light or dark mode. For round 6 the maintainer chose a summary and a timeline for the pane beside the list, and agreed a Description list for the summary and a Timeline for the activity.

Sections 1 to 4 are general components in `packages/ui`. [Clinical components](#clinical-components) follow them.

## 5. Screens

Layouts filled with synthetic content. They live in the example apps and are not shipped.

### Features being prototyped

Five features are being prototyped as screens. Each is prototyped first in Claude Design with the components that already exist. Where a prototype falls back on generic markup, it shows which proposed component is needed, and that component is then agreed and built, from the bottom up.

| Feature                       | What it is for                                                                                                                  | Proposed components it needs                                                                                                                                               |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Patient list                  | Shared and live, for ward rounds, audits and searches                                                                           | Patient list, Patient row, Presence, Change indicator, Filter bar, Combobox, Command, Comment thread, Task item, Avatar                                                    |
| Patient journey               | A stay from arrival to discharge                                                                                                | Patient journey, Timeline, and a Track chart mark that spans a period                                                                                                      |
| Patient and encounter summary | What has happened, including over the last 24 hours, with notes, results, reports and observations                              | Patient banner, Last 24 hours, Description list, Accordion, Note item, Note view, Report view, Problem list, Allergy list. Results over time may be the Observation table. |
| Discharge summary             | Drafted by AI, then reviewed and completed by a doctor, possibly working from a list                                            | Document editor, AI provenance, Section review, Diff view, Stepper, Progress, Sign-off dialog                                                                              |
| Unit dashboard                | For patients and team members: workload across a unit's teams, patient acuity, and what is blocking progress, such as discharge | Stat, Bar chart, Meter, Bed board, Team member row, Blocker list                                                                                                           |

## Clinical components

Subject-matter components in `packages/clinical`, built on the general ones above. Each is render-only: it displays an interpretation it is given and holds no threshold ([decision 0002](decisions/0002-clinical-logic-boundary.md)). Each arrives with its view model in `core` and its FHIR transform in `fhir`, and is agreed one at a time. Each is graded under the matrix in [decision 0004](decisions/0004-component-grading.md).

| Component         | Status   | Level     | Built on                                                        | Shows                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------- | -------- | --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Observation table | Built    | Pattern   | Table                                                           | A flowsheet: one row per series, one column per round of readings, with each value's band and its change, and the source's own interpretation read out beside the band.                                                                                   |
| Vitals chart      | Built    | Pattern   | Track chart, Tabs, Dropdown menu, Tooltip, Button               | Vital signs on stacked tracks over a linear time axis, with bands from a schema, span presets, a track filter and three y-range modes. The Observation table is its equivalent without the chart.                                                         |
| Medication card   | Built    | Composite | Card, Badge, Collapsible, Button                                | One medicine: name, dose, route, frequency and status, expanding to its dose history and when it was last given. For an order with its administrations, for a medication list, and in words for a patient.                                                |
| Patient banner    | Proposed | Composite | Description list, Badge                                         | Who the patient is: name, identifiers, age and allergies, from `Patient`, `AllergyIntolerance` and `Flag`. Which identifiers it shows, and in what order, follow a published patient identification standard, chosen and cited when the banner is agreed. |
| Patient row       | Proposed | Composite | Badge, Marker                                                   | One patient in a list: who they are, where they are, and the status the list is about.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Task item         | Proposed | Composite | Checkbox, Badge                                                 | One task: what it is, who it is for, its owner and when it is due, from `Task`.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Note item         | Proposed | Composite | Item, Badge                                                     | One clinical note in a list: its type, author, time and status, including amended or entered in error, from `DocumentReference`.                                                                                                                          |
| Note view         | Proposed | Composite | Typeset, Badge                                                  | One note in full, with its author, status and amendments.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Report view       | Proposed | Composite | Typeset, Table                                                  | A diagnostic report with its results and conclusion, from `DiagnosticReport`.                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Problem list      | Proposed | Composite | Item, Badge                                                     | Conditions with their status and onset, from `Condition`.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Allergy list      | Proposed | Composite | Item, Badge                                                     | Allergies and intolerances with the substance, reaction and criticality as recorded, from `AllergyIntolerance`.                                                                                                                                           |
| Team member row   | Proposed | Composite | Avatar, Meter                                                   | A clinician, their role and the patients assigned to them, from `PractitionerRole` and `CareTeam`.                                                                                                                                                        |
| Blocker list      | Proposed | Composite | Item, Badge                                                     | What is holding up a patient's progress, such as discharge: each blocker, its owner and how long it has stood, from `Task` or `Flag`.                                                                                                                     |
| Sign-off dialog   | Proposed | Composite | Dialog, Form                                                    | Signing a clinical document, stating who signs and what they are signing.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Patient list      | Proposed | Pattern   | Data table, Patient row, Presence, Change indicator, Filter bar | The shared, live list used for ward rounds, audits and searches, from `Patient`, `Encounter` and `Location`.                                                                                                                                              |
| Patient journey   | Proposed | Pattern   | Timeline, Track chart                                           | A stay from arrival to discharge: each location in its lane on the Track chart's time axis, and the events on a Timeline, from `Encounter`, `EpisodeOfCare` and `Location`. The lanes need a new Track chart mark that spans a period.                    |
| Last 24 hours     | Proposed | Pattern   | Timeline, Note item, Observation table                          | What happened to a patient over a stated period: notes, results, reports, observations and medicines.                                                                                                                                                     |
| Bed board         | Proposed | Pattern   | Patient row, Badge                                              | A unit's beds and who is in each, from `Location` and `Encounter`. Acuity is shown as it is given: Cadence calculates none.                                                                                                                               |

The FHIR transforms are written by hand in `fhir`, and an observation schema, applied outside the component, holds the thresholds and labels ([decision 0016](decisions/0016-fhir-transforms.md)).

More will be added here as they are named. The adult and paediatric observation chart formats are a Region's to supply.
