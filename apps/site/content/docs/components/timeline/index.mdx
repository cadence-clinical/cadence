## Use it for

- A record's activity, such as everything that happened to a patient: comments, notes, results, jobs and changes to their details.
- An audit trail, where who changed what and when must be read in order.
- The steps a request went through, such as a referral sent, seen and accepted.

## Do not use it for

- **The current state of a record.** Use a [Description list](/docs/components/description-list) for the facts as they stand, and keep the timeline for how they came to be.
- **Values compared over time**, such as a run of observations. Use a [Table](/docs/components/table), or a chart such as the [Track chart](/docs/components/track-chart).
- **A thread of comments alone.** Use a [Comment](/docs/components/comment) list, which counts the comments and holds their box.

## Put the newest first in a record's activity

On a ward the latest change is the one most likely to matter next. Say the order in the timeline's name, such as "Activity for Margaret Holloway, newest first", because a screen reader cannot see where the list starts.

## Say the kind, who and when before what

Every item answers four questions in the same order: what kind of thing happened, who did it, when, and what. The header comes first, so a reader scanning down the list, and a screen reader, meet the kind and the author before the detail.

- **Say the kind in words** with `TimelineTitle`. The icon repeats it for the eye and is hidden from a screen reader.
- **Give each kind its own icon**, used for that kind only.
- **Give who in full**, with a role where the reader may not know them.
- **Give the time in the time zone of the ward**, and the day when it is not today.

## Show what is missing as missing

When a source does not say who did something, such as a result with no performer, leave who out rather than guessing. Do not fill the gap with a department or a system name the data does not hold.

## Filter by kind with a toggle group

A long timeline needs a way to show one kind. Use a [Toggle group](/docs/components/toggle-group) above it, with All and one toggle a kind, and treat a released toggle as All, so one filter always shows as pressed. Do not use [Tabs](/docs/components/tabs): tabs switch between panels, and the filter narrows one list. [Examples](/docs/components/timeline/examples#filtered-by-kind) shows the filter.

When an action adds an item the filter hides, such as posting a comment while Notes is pressed, show All, so the new item is in view.

## Keep a comment's own behaviour in the timeline

An item can hold a [Comment](/docs/components/comment): its text, its marks for an edit, a revocation or an AI draft, and its author's actions. The timeline's header carries who and when, so leave the comment's own header out.

## Write what happened

- **Start with a verb in the past tense**: "Changed the expected discharge to Wed 21 Oct, from Tue 20 Oct."
- **Give the value before and after** a change, so the reader does not have to find the earlier item.
- **Show values with their units**, as the source gives them, and never highlight one: the timeline holds no thresholds.

## Accessibility

- **Name the timeline** with `aria-label`, and say its order in the name.
- **Say each item's kind in words.** The icon is hidden from a screen reader.
- **Announce an item added while someone is reading**, such as a teammate's comment, in a live region. A timeline does not announce its changes.
