## Usage

```tsx
import {
  AppShell,
  AppShellBody,
  AppShellContent,
  AppShellHeader,
  AppShellSidebar,
  SidebarContent,
} from "@cadence-clinical/ui";

<AppShell>
  <AppShellHeader>
    <span className="font-medium">Cadence Clinic</span>
  </AppShellHeader>
  <AppShellBody>
    <AppShellSidebar collapsible="icon">
      <SidebarContent aria-label="Clinic">...</SidebarContent>
    </AppShellSidebar>
    <AppShellContent>...</AppShellContent>
  </AppShellBody>
</AppShell>;
```

The Application shell lays a page out as shadcn's [`sidebar-16`](https://ui.shadcn.com/blocks/sidebar#sidebar-16) block does: a header that stays at the top, and a [Sidebar](/docs/components/sidebar) that starts under the header, beside the content. shadcn ships that as a block you copy. Cadence ships the frame, and you keep using the Sidebar's own parts inside it.

The shell is a [layout](/docs/levels): it holds no content of its own. The header's words, the sidebar's items and the page are yours.

## The parts

- **`AppShell`** is the Sidebar's `SidebarProvider`, arranged in a column, and takes its props, such as `defaultOpen`. It starts with a "Skip to content" link, hidden until it takes focus.
- **`AppShellHeader`** is the `header` across the top. It stays in view as the page scrolls. It starts with the button that opens and closes the sidebar, followed by your content.
- **`AppShellBody`** is the row under the header.
- **`AppShellSidebar`** is a `Sidebar`, with all its props, starting under the header. On a phone it is the Sidebar's sheet, over the whole screen.
- **`AppShellContent`** is the page's `main` landmark, where the skip link moves focus to.

The header is as tall as a control plus its padding, so it follows the [density](/docs/theming#density): 48px compact and 68px comfortable.

## What Cadence changes

- **A link skips to the content.** The first Tab on a page shows "Skip to content", which moves focus past the header and the sidebar. It moves focus without changing the page's address, so it does not upset a router.
- **The header always has the sidebar's button.** On a phone, where the sidebar is a sheet, that button is the only way to reach it.
- **The header's height follows the density**, where `sidebar-16` sets it to 56px.

## Accessibility

- The frame gives the page its landmarks: the header is the banner, the Sidebar's content is navigation, and the content is the main landmark. Name the navigation with `aria-label` on `SidebarContent`.
- The skip link is the first thing Tab reaches, and it appears when it has focus.
- The header's button says whether the sidebar is open.
