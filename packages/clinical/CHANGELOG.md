## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- [#83](https://github.com/cadence-clinical/cadence/pull/83) [`8554d11`](https://github.com/cadence-clinical/cadence/commit/8554d1196a4517a2438d7847e1c5d33b382d62bc) Thanks [@ronaldh46](https://github.com/ronaldh46)! - Medication card: the dose history is a table, so times, statuses and doses line up in columns. Why a dose was not given is in a Tooltip on its status badge, which a keyboard reaches, rather than beside it. The route is a badge after the medicine's name, and is no longer repeated in the dose line.

- [#83](https://github.com/cadence-clinical/cadence/pull/83) [`c7c4fd2`](https://github.com/cadence-clinical/cadence/commit/c7c4fd26078d9421214ed831bb308c5508fdd060) Thanks [@ronaldh46](https://github.com/ronaldh46)! - Add Medication card: one medicine, how it is taken in the words of the national on-screen display guidelines, and its status. An order shows when it was last given and whether the latest dose was not given and why, and opens to its doses in the last 24 hours, with earlier doses on request. A "when required" order missing its reason or its maximum in 24 hours says so. With `audience="patient"`, the instruction is in plain words and internal statuses are left out.

- [#83](https://github.com/cadence-clinical/cadence/pull/83) [`fa156e8`](https://github.com/cadence-clinical/cadence/commit/fa156e80dec890cbf50c665837ee5f550d6e1de1) Thanks [@ronaldh46](https://github.com/ronaldh46)! - Observation table: each value's details are in a Tooltip, and the values are one stop for the keyboard, moved through with the arrow keys, Home and End. A Rows menu shows and hides rows, as the Data table's Columns menu does. A last row shows each round's total from `scoreRounds`, with Incomplete and what is missing when a required observation is not there, and the level an escalation raised it to.

- [#83](https://github.com/cadence-clinical/cadence/pull/83) [`42685ba`](https://github.com/cadence-clinical/cadence/commit/42685ba00b8fc710aa9229946d148df67aa04ff7) Thanks [@ronaldh46](https://github.com/ronaldh46)! - Add Observation table, the first clinical component: a flowsheet of observations, one row per series and one column per round, oldest to newest and opening at the newest. Each value shows the band an observation schema gave it by fill, edge, a short label and bold figures, and its change since the value before. A value the schema could not judge says why, a value in another unit carries its own unit, and a value not recorded says so. Its words, times and time zone are passed in.

- [#83](https://github.com/cadence-clinical/cadence/pull/83) [`45cb72a`](https://github.com/cadence-clinical/cadence/commit/45cb72ad0b0cca5c97a88727e93dcac987ba3293) Thanks [@ronaldh46](https://github.com/ronaldh46)! - Observation table: rows are named by their short names, such as RR, by default, with the full name in a tooltip and for a screen reader. Set `labelStyle` to `"full"` to name them in full. A series definition and a total take a `shortLabel`, and `labelFor` gives a name in either style.

- [#83](https://github.com/cadence-clinical/cadence/pull/83) [`81d5d70`](https://github.com/cadence-clinical/cadence/commit/81d5d70d170c59523e1e85afb49095ad8abad9b4) Thanks [@ronaldh46](https://github.com/ronaldh46)! - Add Vitals chart: vital signs on stacked tracks over one time axis, laid out by a `tracks` prop, with the bands an observation schema gave them. Its toolbar chooses the span from 2 hours to 3 days, moves to the start or the latest round, shows and hides tracks, and sets each track's range to the schema's, all values, or the values in view. A value that cannot go on its track's scale is written at its time instead. Track chart's crosshair now snaps to the nearest snap time, and its points take a `pointClassName`.

- [#83](https://github.com/cadence-clinical/cadence/pull/83) [`62264e9`](https://github.com/cadence-clinical/cadence/commit/62264e9104e0825470d68b1adc2b995ed1f42836) Thanks [@ronaldh46](https://github.com/ronaldh46)! - Vitals chart: the pointer over a value opens its Tooltip with its details, and elsewhere draws a line for timing with the time in words beside it. The span and range choices are Tabs. A `totals` track shows each round's total in its level's colour. Track chart: marks take a `detail` for their Tooltip, the chart takes `now` for the time in words, and a band open at an end runs to the track's edge. Adds `friendlyTime`.
